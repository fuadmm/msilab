Laboratory Recruitment & Fellowships

Join Our Immunology Research Group

We welcome passionate and driven students, graduates, and postdoctoral scholars to conduct cutting-edge structural immunology and viral vaccinology research.

Open Positions & Fellowships

Opportunities available across all academic career stages.

01 Full-Time

Doctorate & MPhil

Multi-year investigations into Dengue/JEV ED3 vaccines, antibody neutralization kinetics, and patented peptide solubility tags.

02 MSc Thesis

Postgraduate (MSc)

Master's thesis research involving recombinant protein expression in E. coli, ELISA serology, and FPLC protein purification.

03 Part-Time

Undergraduate Fellows

Hands-on wet lab training for 3rd and 4th-year Biochemistry undergraduates: PCR genotyping, SDS-PAGE, and lab safety protocols.

04 Short-Term

Non-Thesis Internships

Short-term research internships for external scholars seeking intensive training in bioinformatics, structural docking, or viral serology.
