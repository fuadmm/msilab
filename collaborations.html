International Academic

Kuroda Laboratory, Tokyo University of Agriculture & Technology (TUAT) ↗

Tokyo, Japan · Lead Contact: Prof. Dr. Yutaka Kuroda
Department of Biotechnology and Life Science

Long-standing bilateral research collaboration focusing on structural immunology, protein engineering, NMR spectroscopy, X-ray crystallography, and novel peptide solubility tags.

Key Collaborative Scopes:
  • Protein crystallization and Cryo-EM structural resolution
  • Peptide-tagged vaccine candidate formulation
  • Joint MEXT scholar mentorship & dual-lab student exchange
  • Co-invention of international biotechnology patents (WO2019031446)
Joint Patents & 15+ Publications

Chattogram, Bangladesh

Interdisciplinary collaboration investigating indigenous botanical extracts, secondary metabolites, and natural immunomodulatory compounds with antiviral properties.

Key Collaborative Scopes:
  • Screening of native plant extracts against viral surface targets
  • Isolation and structural characterization of phytochemical immunomodulators
  • Joint field specimen collection and bioactivity assays

Clinical Hospital Network

Chittagong Medical College & Hospital (CMCH) ↗

Chattogram, Bangladesh · Directorate General of Health Services
Clinical Pathology & Infectious Disease Units

Premier regional tertiary healthcare center collaborating with MSI Lab on clinical cohort bio-specimen acquisition, acute-phase Dengue and Chikungunya patient serum biobanking, and sero-epidemiological tracking.

Key Collaborative Scopes:
  • Clinical cohort bio-specimen acquisition and clinical diagnostics
  • Acute-phase Dengue and Chikungunya patient serum biobanking
  • Validation of novel diagnostic biomarkers on clinical patient samples
  • Joint clinical-immunological case investigations and cross-disciplinary rounds
