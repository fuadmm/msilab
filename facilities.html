Safety & Standards

Supporting Infrastructure & Safety Environment

Our instrumentation is operated under strict standard operating procedures (SOPs) complying with bio-risk management guidelines.

Biosafety Level 2

BSL-2 Tissue Culture Suite

Certified Class II biosafety cabinets, CO2 incubators, inverted microscopy, and cryogenic storage for viral neutralization and cell assays.

Cold-Chain Integrity

Cryogenic Specimen Biobank

Ultra-low (-80°C) deep freezers, liquid nitrogen storage, and temperature loggers for long-term clinical serum samples and hybridomas.

Bioprocessing

Recombinant Protein Prep

Automated autoclaves, refrigerated centrifuges, orbital shaking incubators, and sterile filtration assemblies for microbial expression.

In Silico Synergy

Structural Modeling Station

GPU-accelerated computing workstations for molecular dynamics (GROMACS), Rosetta de novo protein engineering, and Cryo-EM models.
