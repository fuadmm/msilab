1-Step RT-qPCR Mastermix & Amplification Protocol

Standard operating procedure for one-step real-time reverse transcription PCR, covering RNase-free glassware preparation, primer-probe mastermix assembly, and thermal cycling parameters for viral pathogen surveillance.

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Highlights:
  • ✓ Baking glassware at 180–200°C for ≥4 hours to destroy RNases
  • ✓ TE buffer & DEPC-treated nuclease-free water preparation
  • ✓ Multiplex primer and hydrolysis probe mastermix formulation
  • ✓ qTOWER³ G linear thermal cycling and threshold baseline calibration

CTAB-Based Total RNA Extraction from Recalcitrant Agarwood Heartwood
