## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Antara Saha is a Graduate Research Assistant and Ph.D. Student in the Department of Biochemistry & Biophysics at Texas A&M University, USA. She earned her M.S. and B.Sc. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong. At MSI Lab, she investigated cellular Th1/Th2 cytokine memory and long-term anti-ED3 IgG responses across all four dengue serotypes.
