## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Md. Din Islam is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Graduate Institute of Microbiology and Public Health, National Chung Hsing University (NCHU), Taiwan. He completed his Ph.D. in Veterinary Medicine / Virology at NCHU and his M.S. and B.Sc. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong. His expertise spans virology, oncolytic Orf virus genetic engineering, and dengue subunit vaccine immunology.
