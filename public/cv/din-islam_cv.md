## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Md. Din Islam is an accomplished molecular virologist, protein biochemist, and immunologist currently serving as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Graduate Institute of Microbiology and Public Health, National Chung Hsing University (NCHU), Taiwan. He earned his Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D. / Doctor of Engineering) in Biotechnology and Life Science from Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT), Tokyo, Japan under the mentorship of Prof. Yutaka Kuroda. Prior to his doctoral studies, he obtained his Master of Science (M.S.) and Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. Honours) degrees in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, conducting his master's thesis research at the Laboratory of Molecular and Structural Immunology (MSI Lab) under Prof. Dr. Mohammad Monirul Islam.

His research career bridges fundamental protein engineering, innovative biophysical purification strategies, and viral vaccine development. His primary expertise covers oncolytic Orf virus (ORFV) genetic manipulation, recombinant flavivirus subunit vaccines (DENV, JEV), solubility-controlling peptide tags (SEP tags), metal-assisted selective protein precipitation, and antibody neutralization dynamics.

## 5. Technical Skills & Laboratory Methodologies

* **Recombinant DNA & Molecular Cloning:** PCR, primer design, site-directed mutagenesis, gene synthesis and subcloning into bacterial and viral expression vectors, plasmid preparation, agarose gel electrophoresis.
* **Protein Expression & Engineering:** Heterologous protein expression in *Escherichia coli* and yeast (*Pichia pastoris*), design and incorporation of short peptide solubility tags (SEP tags), inclusion body refolding, and optimization of expression conditions.
* **Protein Purification & Biophysical Characterization:** Ni-NTA affinity chromatography, GST purification, fast protein liquid chromatography (FPLC/HPLC), size-exclusion chromatography (SEC), $\text{Ni}^{2+}$-induced selective precipitation, SDS-PAGE, Western blotting, circular dichroism (CD) spectroscopy, dynamic light scattering (DLS).
* **Immunological Assays & In Vivo Techniques:** Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA: direct, indirect, sandwich, and cross-reactivity profiling), Plaque Reduction Neutralization Tests (PRNT), anti-drug antibody (ADA) generation assessment, mouse handling, subcutaneous and intramuscular immunizations, serum harvesting, and splenocyte isolation.
* **Virology & Oncolytic Viral Platforms:** Oncolytic Orf virus (ORFV) reconstitution, viral plaque assays, TCID50 titration, mammalian cell culture (Vero, HeLa, primary cells), viral transduction and replication kinetics.
* **Bioinformatics & Computational Tools:** SnapGene, PyMOL, GraphPad Prism, NCBI BLAST, Swiss-PdbViewer, Clustal Omega, MEGA.

## 8. Primary Research Interests
* **Recombinant Subunit Vaccine Design:** Developing cost-effective, high-yield *E. coli* platforms for expressing viral antigens (DENV, JEV, Influenza, Coronaviruses) preserving native-like immunogenic folds.
* **Solubility & Aggregation Control:** Engineering short peptide tags (SEP tags) to modulate protein solubility, colloidal stability, and self-adjuvanting aggregate formation.
* **Non-Chromatographic Bioseparations:** Designing metal ion-assisted precipitation techniques ($\text{Ni}^{2+}$, $\text{Co}^{2+}$) for rapid, cost-effective recombinant protein recovery.
* **Oncolytic Virotherapy & Cancer Immunotherapy:** Engineering recombinant Orf viruses (ORFV) encoding immunostimulatory ligands to treat refractory malignancies.
