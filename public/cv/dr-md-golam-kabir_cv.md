## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Prof. Dr. Md. Golam Kabir is a Professor in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong. He obtained his Ph.D. in Biotechnology and Life Science from Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT), Japan (2018). His research encompasses pathogenesis, monoclonal antibody engineering against viral envelope antigens, life sciences, and nutritional biochemistry.
