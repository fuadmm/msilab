## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Moushumi Ghosh Roy is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Pharmacy at BGC Trust University Bangladesh. She completed her Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Biotechnology at Lovely Professional University, India, and her M.S. and B.Sc. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong. Her research focuses on dengue clinical epidemiology, outbreak sero-prevalence, co-circulating serotypes in Bangladesh, and subunit vaccine immunogenicity.
