## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Shamsun Nahar Binte Mannan is a Doctoral Fellow (Ph.D. Candidate) at MSI Lab, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. She serves as Associate Professor & Head of the Department of Skin & VD at Chattogram Maa-O-Shishu Hospital Medical College. Her doctoral investigation focuses on multiplex PCR-based molecular detection of sexually transmitted infections in pregnant women.
