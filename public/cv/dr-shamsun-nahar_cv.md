## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Shamsun Nahar Bintha Mannan is an Associate Professor & Head of the Department of Dermatology & Venereology at Chattogram Maa-O-Shishu Hospital Medical College (CMOSHMC) and a Doctoral Fellow (Ph.D. Candidate) at the Laboratory of Molecular & Structural Immunology (MSI Lab), Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. With more than two decades of distinguished clinical practice, higher academic instruction, and biomedical research in dermatology, venereology, and public health, her doctoral investigation pioneers multiplex PCR-based molecular detection and diagnostic efficacy for sexually transmitted infections (STIs) in vulnerable maternal cohorts.

Her clinical and academic expertise encompasses molecular diagnostics of mucosal and dermatological pathogens, cutaneous mycology with emerging antifungal resistance, neonatal and pediatric dermatoses, psychodermatological disorders, aesthetic dermatology, trichology, and adverse drug reaction pharmacovigilance.

## 2. Doctoral Investigation (Ph.D. Research)
* **Doctoral Thesis Title:** *"Multiplex PCR-Based Molecular Detection of Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) in Pregnant Women: Diagnostic Efficacy and Maternal-Fetal Clinical Implications"*
* **Laboratory:** Laboratory of Molecular & Structural Immunology (MSI Lab), Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong
* **Principal Supervisor:** Professor Mohammad Monirul Islam, PhD (MSI Lab, CU)
* **Co-Supervisor:** Dr. Md. Golam Kabir (Associate Professor, Department of Dermatology, CMOSHMC)
* **Status:** Ongoing Doctoral Research (Ph.D. Candidate)
* **Scientific Focus:** Evaluating the clinical diagnostic efficacy, analytical sensitivity, specificity, and cost-effectiveness of multiplex real-time/multiplex PCR assays against conventional microbiological cultures and serological methods for early detection of *Chlamydia trachomatis*, *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*, *Trichomonas vaginalis*, *Mycoplasma genitalium*, and Herpes Simplex Virus in high-risk pregnant women to prevent adverse pregnancy and neonatal outcomes.

* **Therapeutic & Procedural Competencies:** Liquid nitrogen cryosurgery, radiofrequency electrocautery, intralesional triamcinolone acetonide infiltration for keloids and alopecia, superficial to medium chemical peeling (salicylic acid, glycolic acid, Jessner's solution, trichloroacetic acid), acne scar subcision and microneedling, autologous Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) processing and clinical intradermal injection, hair transplantation harvesting and graft placement, phototherapy (narrowband UVB) dosimetry, and surgical excision of benign skin tumors, cysts, and moles.
