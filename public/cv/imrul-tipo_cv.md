## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Imrul Hasan Tipo is a Lecturer in the Department of Biochemistry and Biotechnology at University of Science and Technology Chittagong (USTC) and a Ph.D. Fellow in Biological Sciences at the University of Alabama, USA. He received his BSc (Hons) and MS (Thesis) in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong. He has co-authored significant papers in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences and medRxiv on dengue vaccine immunology and pandemic transmission dynamics.
