## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Muhammad Mamunur Rashid Mahib is an Associate Professor of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong. He earned his Ph.D. from Kanazawa University, Japan (2020) and completed postdoctoral training at MaineHealth Institute for Research, USA. His research focuses on cellular signaling pathways, innate immune signaling, cell death (apoptosis and necroptosis), and infectious disease pathogenesis.
