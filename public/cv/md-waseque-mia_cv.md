## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Md. Waseque Mia is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet. He completed his M.S. and B.Sc. (Hons) at the University of Chittagong, conducting research on Japanese Encephalitis Virus (JEV) ED3 protein engineering at MSI Lab. His academic focus spans immunology, cancer cell biology, molecular epidemiology, and public health risk assessments.
