## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Minhajur Rahman is an Associate Professor in the Department of Botany at the University of Chittagong and a Doctoral Fellow at the Laboratory of Molecular & Structural Immunology (MSI Lab). With over a decade of university teaching and research experience, he specializes in pharmacognosy, natural product chemistry, in silico pharmacology, and phytopharmacology. He has secured multiple competitive research grants from the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST) and CU as Principal Investigator, publishing over 25 peer-reviewed articles and book chapters.
