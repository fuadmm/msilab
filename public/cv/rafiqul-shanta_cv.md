## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Md. Rafiqul Islam Shanta is a Research Fellow at the Bangladesh Forest Research Institute (BFRI). He earned his M.S. and B.Sc. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong. His research at MSI Lab investigated clinical antibody waning kinetics and booster vaccination strategies in Sinopharm-vaccinated human populations.
