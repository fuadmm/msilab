## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Sabrina Akter is a Lecturer in the Department of Biochemistry and Biotechnology at University of Science and Technology Chittagong (USTC). She completed her Master's and Bachelor's (Honours) degrees in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong. Prior to joining USTC, she worked as a Research Associate at the Laboratory of Molecular & Structural Immunology (MSI Lab), focusing on coronavirus variant cross-neutralization.
