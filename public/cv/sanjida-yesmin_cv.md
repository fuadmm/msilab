## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Dr. Sanjida Yesmin is a Postdoctoral / Ph.D. Researcher in Protein Engineering and Molecular Immunology at Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT), Japan, supported by the Japanese Government (MEXT) scholarship. Her research investigates antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE), neutralizing antibody epitope mapping, solubility-controlling peptide tags, and prime-boost vaccination strategies against flaviviruses.
