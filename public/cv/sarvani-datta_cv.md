## 1. Executive Summary & Research Profile
Sarvani Datta is a Graduate Research Assistant at MSI Lab, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. Her MS research evaluates artificial agarwood resin induction in Aquilaria trees by examining both the anatomical extent of resin deposition and the resulting chemical composition under combined biotic and abiotic stresses, identifying quality-defining sesquiterpenes and chromones via GC-MS.
