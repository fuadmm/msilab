Academic Biography

Prof. Dr. Mohammad Monirul Islam leads the Laboratory of Molecular & Structural Immunology (MSI Lab) at the University of Chittagong. With over two decades of experience in structural biology, molecular immunology, and protein technology, his research focuses on understanding the biophysical mechanisms governing host-pathogen interactions, vaccine development against emerging tropical viruses (Dengue, SARS-CoV-2, JEV), and patented peptide tags for solubility and immunogenicity control.

Education & Qualifications:

Ph.D. in Biotechnology Structural Immunology

Tokyo University of Agriculture & Tech (TUAT), Japan (2005–2009)

M.Sc. in Biochemistry Molecular Biology

University of Dhaka, Bangladesh (1999–2000)

B.Sc. (Hons) in Biochemistry Biochemistry & Mol. Biology

University of Dhaka, Bangladesh (1995–1999)

• Professor, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong
• Lab-In-Charge, COVID-19 Testing Lab (over 100,000 RT-PCR molecular tests curated)
• Head, Animal Ethics Review Board, University of Chittagong
• Principal Investigator, World Bank / HEAT Advanced Vaccine Research Grant
